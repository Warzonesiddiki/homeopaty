## 1. Business Model & Strategy (B2B SaaS for Clinicians)

Similimum AI employs a **Freemium B2B subscription model** targeted directly at licensed homeopathic doctors, clinics, and medical colleges. The doctor (not the patient) is the paying customer.

## 3. Pricing Logic & Economic Justification for the Doctor

- **Return on Investment (ROI)**:
  - An average Indian private homeopath charges between ₹300 and ₹1,500 per chronic consultation.
  - At ₹799/month, saving just **one consultation's worth of time** (15 minutes/case across 20 cases = 5 hours saved/month) or completing **one additional patient consultation** covers the entire monthly subscription cost.
- **Comparison to Legacy Alternatives**:
  - Legacy desktop software (e.g. RADAR Opus) charges ₹1,20,000–₹2,50,000 upfront. Similimum AI Pro requires zero upfront capex and delivers modern ambient AI for ₹7,999/year.

## 4. Payment Infrastructure & Compliance
- **Google Play In-App Billing**: Seamless recurring subscription management for Indian debit cards, credit cards, UPI AutoPay, and net banking.
- **GST Invoicing**: Automated GST tax invoices issued for Indian clinic tax deductions.

`[ASSUMPTION-MONETIZE-01]` Google Play's 15% service fee for the first $1M USD annual revenue is factored into unit economics, yielding an 85% net margin on digital subscriptions.
