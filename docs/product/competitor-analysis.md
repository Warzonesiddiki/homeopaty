## 1. Competitive Landscape Overview

The homeopathic software landscape consists of legacy desktop repertorization tools built in the 1990s and early 2000s, alongside a few web-based expert systems. None provide real-time ambient acoustic consultation listening on mobile/tablet devices.

## 3. Similimum AI’s Unique Value Proposition (The "Moat")

1. **Ambient Consulting Desk Presence**: Doctors never have to type or hunt for rubrics while looking at a patient. The app transcribes and maps symptoms into rubrics passively.
2. **Boenninghausen LSMC Completeness Guard**: Prevents incomplete prescriptions by proactively alerting the doctor to missing modalities before a remedy is chosen.
3. **Vernacular Hindi & Hinglish Translation Engine**: Built specifically for the Indian AYUSH market, translating everyday phrases (*"gusse ke baad pet kharab"*, *"dhoop mein sar dard"*) into canonical Kent rubrics.
4. **Offline Resilience with Hybrid Cloud Intelligence**: Runs complete repertorizations in <15ms offline, while unlocking deep Gemini 2.5 Flash constitutional synthesis when internet is accessible.
5. **Zero Compromise Decision Support Framing**: Clean, legal, and ethical non-prescriptive framing that protects the doctor’s autonomy.

`[ASSUMPTION-COMP-01]` Legacy desktop software pricing ($1,000+) creates a massive untapped market of younger and rural practitioners seeking mobile-first subscriptions (under ₹1,000/mo).
