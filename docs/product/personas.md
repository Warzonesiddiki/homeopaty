## Persona 1: Dr. Anand Mehta — The High-Volume Classical Practitioner

- **Age / Experience**: 46 years old | 20 years in classical practice (MD-Hom, Mumbai).
- **Clinic Setting**: Busy private clinic seeing 35–45 patients per day; mix of chronic auto-immune cases and acute pediatric infections.
- **Tech Literacy**: Moderate (uses WhatsApp, basic EHR on laptop for accounting, prefers Android tablet for clinical reading).
- **Core Frustrations**:
  - Takes 30–45 minutes per new chronic case taking paper notes; clinic runs late every evening.
  - Constantly flipping between synthetic repertories on PC or heavy physical books (Kent, Boericke) while patients sit waiting.
  - Misses subtle modalities due to mental fatigue during evening shifts.
- **Goals with Similimum AI**:
  - Wants the app on a tablet stand beside the desk listening quietly without distraction.
  - Glance at high-yield questions to uncover missing modalities without breaking patient eye contact.
  - Instant repertory ranking without manual rubric hunting.

## Persona 2: Dr. Priya Sharma — The Modern Evidence-Based Young Doctor

- **Age / Experience**: 28 years old | 3 years post-BHMS, practicing at an integrative wellness clinic in Bengaluru.
- **Clinic Setting**: Tech-savvy urban demographic; patients expect rapid digital records and scientific explanations.
- **Tech Literacy**: Very High (iPhone/Android flagship user, uses Notion, digital stylus, online medical journals).
- **Core Frustrations**:
  - Struggling to differentiate closely competing polychrests (e.g., *Natrum Mur* vs *Ignatia* vs *Sepia* in grief cases).
  - Wants exact keynote verification and miasmatic profiling before deciding on the final potency.
  - Needs structured case exports that can be shared with senior mentors for case discussions.
- **Goals with Similimum AI**:
  - Use head-to-head Materia Medica differentials with keynote checklists.
  - Accurate LM potency dilution guidance for sensitive allergic patients.
  - Strict decision-support backing with classical source literature citations.

## Persona 3: Dr. Rameshwar Patil — The Rural / Semi-Urban Traveling Homeopath

- **Age / Experience**: 58 years old | 32 years practice in semi-urban and rural Maharashtra.
- **Clinic Setting**: Rural dispensary and traveling outreach clinics; sees 60+ patients daily in vernacular Marathi and Hindi.
- **Tech Literacy**: Low to Moderate (relies on affordable 4G Android phone; dislikes complex multi-nested menus).
- **Connectivity Reality**: Frequent power cuts and spotty 3G/4G network in rural outposts; cloud-only apps are unusable.
- **Core Frustrations**:
  - Cannot use heavy cloud-reliant software because mobile internet disconnects unpredictably.
  - Vernacular patient descriptions (local Hindi/Marathi dialects) need immediate mapping to standard remedies.
  - Inadvertent prescription of inimical remedies by junior compounders.
- **Goals with Similimum AI**:
  - 100% offline functionality for repertory and patient records.
  - 1-tap silent observation chips (e.g., *Restless*, *Thirstless*) that work instantly without typing.
  - Prominent safety blocker preventing dangerous drug combinations (*Apis* vs *Rhus Tox*).
