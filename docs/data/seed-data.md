## 1. Classical Polychrest Remedies Seed Catalog (30 Core Polychrests)

The application ships with a pre-seeded, on-device clinical repository containing complete Materia Medica monographs for 30 classical polychrests:

1. **Aconitum Napellus (`Acon`)**: Acute violent sudden onset from dry cold wind; intense panic, restless anguish, unquenchable cold thirst.
2. **Apis Mellifica (`Apis`)**: Stinging burning pains, puffiness and edema, thirstless, extreme heat aggravation (< heat), cold amelioration (> cold).
3. **Argentum Nitricum (`Arg-n`)**: Anticipatory anxiety, time passes too slowly, craving for sweets which aggravate, splinters in throat.
4. **Arnica Montana (`Arn`)**: Bruised sore lame sensation, beds feel too hard, claims nothing is wrong after physical trauma.
5. **Arsenicum Album (`Ars`)**: Rapid prostration, burning pains relieved by heat, midnight aggravation (1–2 AM), thirst for small sips, fastidious restlessness.
6. **Belladonna (`Bell`)**: Violent sudden throbbing, flushed red face, dilated pupils, delirium, hypersensitive to light, noise, touch, and jarring.
7. **Bryonia Alba (`Bry`)**: Stitching tearing pains, extreme aggravation from slightest motion, ameliorated by lying on painful side, thirst for large drinks at long intervals.
8. **Calcarea Carbonica (`Calc`)**: Chilly, cold damp feet, head sweats during sleep, craving for boiled eggs, easily exhausted by exertion.
9. **Carbo Vegetabilis (`Carbo-v`)**: State of collapse, sluggish venous circulation, cold breath, air hunger demanding continuous fanning.
10. **Causticum (`Caust`)**: Sympathetic nature, ailments from grief/injustice, involuntary urination on coughing, better in damp wet weather.
11. **Chamomilla (`Cham`)**: Unbearable irritability, one cheek hot and red while other is pale and cool, demanding to be carried.
12. **China Officinalis (`Chin`)**: Debility from loss of vital fluids, periodic fever, extreme hypersensitivity to touch yet relieved by hard pressure.
13. **Gelsemium Sempervirens (`Gels`)**: Motor paralysis, dullness, dizziness, drowsiness, trembling from anticipation, complete thirstlessness.
14. **Hepar Sulphuris (`Hep`)**: Hypersensitive to cold air and light touch, sharp splinter-like pains, violent temper, ameliorated by moist warmth.
15. **Ignatia Amara (`Ign`)**: Paradoxical symptoms, acute recent grief, silent brooding, frequent deep sighing, headache relieved by hard pressure.
16. **Kali Carbonicum (`Kali-c`)**: Stitching pains, 2–4 AM aggravation, puffiness of upper eyelids, backache requiring firm support.
17. **Lachesis Mutus (`Lach`)**: Left to right progression, loquacious, intolerant of tight collars/waistbands, worse after sleep, suspicious.
18. **Lycopodium Clavatum (`Lyc`)**: Right to left, 4–8 PM aggravation, severe flatulence/bloating, craving for warm drinks, intellectual yet timid.
19. **Mercurius Solubilis (`Merc`)**: Nocturnal bone pains, profuse offensive night sweats that give no relief, metallic taste, sensitive to both heat and cold.
20. **Natrum Muriaticum (`Nat-m`)**: Silent chronic grief, worse 10 AM–3 PM sun, mapped tongue, craving for salt, aversion to consolation.
21. **Nux Vomica (`Nux-v`)**: Sedentary, ambitious, irritable, overindulgence in stimulants/coffee/spices, ineffectual urging for stool, chilly.
22. **Phosphorus (`Phos`)**: Tall slender, empathetic, craving for ice-cold drinks, burning spine and palms, easy bleeding, worse thunderstorms.
23. **Pulsatilla Pratensis (`Puls`)**: Mild, yielding, tearful, better with gentle consolation, complete thirstlessness, marked relief in open cool air.
24. **Rhus Toxicodendron (`Rhus-t`)**: Restless stiffness, red triangular tip on tongue, worse beginning motion and cold damp, better continued motion and heat.
25. **Sepia Officinalis (`Sep`)**: Indifference to loved ones, pelvic bearing-down sensation, brownish saddle over nose, better vigorous exercise.
26. **Silicea Terra (`Sil`)**: Lack of physical/mental grit, chilly, profuse offensive foot sweat, suppurative tendency, splinter sensations.
27. **Staphysagria (`Staph`)**: Ailments from suppressed anger, indignity, wounded pride, trembling with fury, honeymoon cystitis.
28. **Sulphur (`Sulph`)**: Philosophical, burning on vertex and soles at night, 11 AM empty sinking stomach, aversion to washing, skin eruptions.
29. **Thuja Occidentalis (`Thuj`)**: Sycotic hydrogenoid diathesis, warty excrescences, fixed somatic delusions, worse damp cold and 3 AM.
30. **Zincum Metallicum (`Zinc`)**: Fidgety restless feet, brain exhaustion, convulsions, suppressed eruptions leading to spinal twitching.
